Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything founders and marketers ask before joining.

What exactly does CAD do?+

CAD shows you where your customers are leaving your website and finds the problem behind it. If 500 visitors bring you 10 purchases, CAD tells you what the other 490 saw, where they stalled, and why they didn't buy — so you can fix it and grow revenue.

How is CAD different from Google Analytics or Hotjar?+

Analytics tools show you numbers — sessions, bounce rate, conversion. Session tools show you recordings — and leave the diagnosis to you. CAD is the diagnosis layer: an agent connected to your analytics names the exact problem and tells you how to solve it. Answers, not more charts.

Do I need a developer to set it up?+

No. All you do is get a code from the CAD system and paste it into your website. That's the whole setup — about 2 minutes, no developer, no configuration.

I'm not technical. Will I actually understand the data?+

Yes — that's the point. The agent works like your own data analyst, at your place: it reads what customers do and explains it in plain language. As a founder or marketer, you see where your sales are going in an easy, simple way.

Can I chat with the AI about my own store's data?+

Yes. The AI is connected to your website's live data — ask things like “when do most people purchase”, “where are my sales dipping” or “what should I push more”, and it answers in plain language, from your numbers.

How accurate is the data I'm seeing?+

CAD tracks real visitor actions on your website — every click, scroll and checkout step. The AI answers only from what actually happened in your store, so what you see is simple, stable and precise — accurate enough to base decisions on.
